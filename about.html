    Our Story

    Deep Roots in Real Estate Finance

    CBiz Capital was built on a foundation of genuine expertise — not just deal volume. Our principals have been actively working in real estate finance, institutional lending, private money, construction, and development since 1986, giving us a perspective on the market that only comes from decades of hands-on experience.

    We understand that every deal is different. Market conditions change. Timelines compress. Properties have unique challenges. Our job is to understand the full picture — not fit your deal into a box — and structure financing that actually works for the investor in front of us.

    From single-family rentals to multifamily portfolios, fix-and-flip rehabs to time-sensitive bridge scenarios, we bring the same level of attention and expertise to every transaction.
