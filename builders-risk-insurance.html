    Coverage Details

    Comprehensive Protection

    Builders Risk Insurance is designed to protect your project at every stage — from groundbreaking to certificate of occupancy.

    Structure Coverage

    Protects the building itself — framing, walls, roof, and all permanent fixtures — while under active construction or renovation.

    Materials & Equipment

    Covers building materials stored on-site or in transit, plus equipment and tools used in the construction process.

    Fire & Weather

    Coverage for fire, lightning, wind, hail, and other weather-related events that can cause significant damage mid-project.

    Theft & Vandalism

    Protects against theft of materials and equipment, and vandalism to work already completed on the structure.

    Soft Costs

    Optional coverage for soft costs like architectural fees, permits, and financing costs if a covered loss causes project delays.

    Customizable Limits

    Coverage limits tailored to your project's completed value — from small single-family rehabs to large multifamily builds.
