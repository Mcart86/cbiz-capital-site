    Commercial Financing Disclosure

    CBizCapital arranges commercial financing, including commercial mortgages and construction loans, for business purposes only. CBizCapital is not a residential mortgage broker/lender and this Site does not offer consumer mortgage products.

    About This Policy

    This Privacy Policy explains how CBizCapital ("CBizCapital," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit cbizcapital.com (the "Site") or contact us through it. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

    Information We Collect

    Information you give us. When you fill out a form, call, or email us, we collect what you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, and details about your property or financing request.

    Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, we and our service providers collect technical and usage information through cookies and similar technologies, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the links you click, and the site that referred you.

    How We Use Information

    • To respond to your inquiries and evaluate or arrange the financing you ask about
    • To follow up with you about your request and our loan programs
    • To understand how the Site is used and to improve it
    • To measure and manage our marketing
    • To protect the Site and comply with legal obligations

    Third-Party Services

    We use third-party services to run the Site and manage inquiries. These providers process information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies:

    • Google Analytics to measure Site traffic and how visitors use the Site, including clicks on our "Apply Now" links.
    • HubSpot for website tracking and for managing contacts and follow-up.
    • Formspree to process submissions from our contact form.

    The "Apply Now" links take you to a separate application site (credealapp.com). Information you submit there is handled under that site's own terms and privacy practices, not this policy. Our social media links also lead to third-party sites with their own policies.

    Cookies and Your Choices

    You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the Site may not work as intended. To opt out of Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. To stop receiving our emails, use the unsubscribe link in the message or contact us.

    How We Share Information

    We share information with the service providers described above; with lenders, capital partners, and other parties involved as needed to evaluate or arrange financing you request; and when required by law or to protect our rights, our users, or others. If CBizCapital is involved in a merger, sale, or similar transaction, information may transfer as part of it.

    Security and Retention

    We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or transmission method is completely secure. We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

    Requests About Your Information

    To ask what information we hold about you, or to request a correction or deletion where applicable, contact us using the details below.

    Children

    The Site is intended for business and investor use and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
