    Benefits

    Why Investors Use Small Balance Multifamily Loans

    Purpose-built multifamily financing for investors who want to scale beyond single-family without the complexity of large commercial loans.

    Income-Based Qualification

    Loans are underwritten on the property's rental income and DSCR — not your personal W-2 or employment history.

    Entity Ownership

    Close in an LLC or entity structure — protecting assets and keeping your portfolio organized.

    Cash-Out Available

    Pull equity from stabilized multifamily assets to fund your next acquisition or portfolio expansion.

    Faster Than Banks

    Traditional banks are slow and rigid. We move faster and underwrite based on the asset — not a bureaucratic checklist.

    Interest-Only Options

    Preserve cash flow with interest-only payment structures available on select programs.

    Clear Timeline

    Know where you stand from day one. We provide preliminary terms within 24–48 hours and keep you informed throughout.
